# scrnaseq-NPC
Nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC) is highly immune infiltrated, yet antitumor T-cell activity is often impaired. We integrated single-cell RNA sequencing of 374,610 cells from 25 NPC and nine nonmalignant tissues with spatial transcriptomics and external cohorts to examine how malignant-cell heterogeneity relates to T-cell dysfunction. Four reproducible malignant-cell programs were identified. MP2 combined antigen-presentation machinery with immunoregulatory signals and was associated at the patient level with exhausted CD8⁺ T cells, regulatory T cells, and advanced-stage disease. Spatial analyses showed that MP3 marked a CD8-enriched inflammatory environment containing both cytotoxic and exhausted T-cell states. MP2 was not independently associated with local T-cell exhaustion after adjustment for MP3; instead, higher MP2 activity was associated with increased exhaustion and reduced cytotoxicity specifically in MP3-high regions. LGALS9 was enriched in MP2-high malignant cells, and Galectin-9 blockade reduced exhaustion-associated checkpoint expression and enhanced T-cell-mediated tumor killing in vitro.
